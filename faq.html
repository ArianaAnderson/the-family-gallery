Questions and answers

What to know before you purchase.

Do I need a special art television?

No. USB ports have been a common television feature for roughly the past decade. The USB is intended for televisions that can display JPEG photos from USB storage. Look for a Photos, Media Player, Gallery, or USB option in your TV menu.

Will it work on every smart TV?

Compatibility depends on the specific television. Many TVs support USB photo playback, but not every model provides the same features. Check your manual before ordering if you are uncertain.

Is there a monthly subscription?

No. The Family Gallery TV USB is a one-time physical purchase. There is no Family Gallery app or recurring fee.

How many artworks are included?

The USB includes more than 600 captioned works of classic art.

Are the paintings identified?

Yes. Every prepared image includes the artwork title and artist on a caption bar.

Can the collection help my family learn about art?

Yes. Each caption gives you the artwork title and artist as a starting point. Families can talk about what they notice, and adults who never studied art history can explore at their own pace. You do not need any background in art to begin.

How is the artwork chosen?

The collection focuses on European, Russian, and American artworks created before 1910. Each work is reviewed for family-friendly display, and the collection excludes nudity and graphic violence or gore.

Are the paintings cropped to fill the television?

No. Vermeer did not paint on a 16:9 television-shaped canvas, so we do not cut away part of his art to make it fit one. Every painting is preserved in its original proportions and displayed with a coordinated background when needed. You see the artist’s complete composition—not a cropped or stretched version.

Does it need Wi-Fi?

The USB itself does not need Wi-Fi. It is read locally by the television’s photo or media player.

How fast should the slideshow change?

We suggest beginning with 45–90 seconds per image, then adjusting for your room and preference.

Where do I purchase it?

Orders and payment are completed through The Family Gallery TV’s Etsy listing. For order questions, email orders@familygallerytv.com.

What if I need help?

Email help@familygallerytv.com and include your order number and television brand/model when possible.
