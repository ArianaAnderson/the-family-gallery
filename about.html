Our founder and origin story

A more beautiful use for the television you already own.

The Family Gallery TV was created to make classic art more accessible—without asking families to buy an expensive specialty television or add another monthly subscription.

How it began

Beauty should not require replacing what already works.

The idea began with a simple question: why should bringing art into a home require replacing a perfectly good television? Our founder wanted a practical, budget-friendly way to turn the screen already in the room into something beautiful, useful, and educational.

That idea became The Family Gallery TV: a carefully prepared USB collection of more than 1000 classic works, each captioned with the artwork title and artist. Insert it into a compatible television, start a slideshow, and the familiar black screen becomes a changing home gallery.

Using an existing television can avoid an unnecessary hardware purchase and the waste that comes with replacing a working device. Because the collection is stored locally, it also works without a Family Gallery subscription, advertisements, or a constant internet connection.

What guides us

Art that fits real homes and real life.

Use what you own

A compatible television can become an art display without the cost and waste of replacing a working screen.

Pay once

The collection is purchased once and stored on the USB—no Family Gallery subscription, advertisements, or recurring fee.

Know what you see

Every image includes the title and artist, turning everyday viewing into an invitation to recognize, discuss, and explore.

Who is our customer?

For anyone who wants more art in everyday life.

The Family Gallery TV is made for families, curious adults, home decorators, and thoughtful gift-givers who value beauty, learning, simplicity, and a sensible price.

It is especially welcoming to adults who never had the chance to study art history—or whose lives simply took them in another direction. No prior knowledge is expected. The captions make it easy to begin recognizing artists and works naturally, one picture at a time.

For families, the changing artwork can prompt conversation and shared discovery. For design-minded households, it offers a calmer alternative to a blank television. And as a gift, it is useful, distinctive, and ready to enjoy on a compatible TV.
