Why this exists

Art-TV presence without the Art-TV bill.

Most televisions default to noise. A Frame or Art TV fixes that by turning the set into a hanging gallery — then often asks for a new panel and a store subscription. The Family Gallery is the other option: a chosen set of pre-1910 paintings, finished so they can hang on the television already in the room.

There is no subscription. There is no Art Store login. You buy the USB or the download once. The files stay yours.

The works come from public-domain museum collections. The hours are in the choosing, the family-safe edit, the captions, and the files that actually fill a modern screen.

Not affiliated with Samsung or The Frame. We sell the gallery, not the hardware.
