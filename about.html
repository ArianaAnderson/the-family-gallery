Why this exists

A screen that makes the room better.

Most televisions default to noise. The Family Gallery is the other option: a chosen set of pre-1910 paintings, finished so they can hang on the biggest wall in the house without anyone standing guard over the remote.

The works come from public-domain museum collections. The hours are in the choosing, the family-safe edit, the captions, and the files that actually fill a modern screen.

There is no account, no feed, and no plan to rent the paintings back to you every month.
