Why your TV’s black screen dominates the room: three fixes

Try art playback, a more considered TV arrangement or a deliberate off-screen choice to help the television fit your living room.

Published October 6, 2026 · The Family Gallery

A large dark rectangle draws the eye when the rest of the room is soft, colorful or full of natural materials. If a blank television bothers you, there are several ways to help it fit the space. You do not have to redesign the whole room to change what you notice first.

1. Give the screen a second purpose

On a TV with USB JPEG slideshow support, display paintings when you want the room to have a different focus. Start with a landscape or garden whose colors relate to the room. Keep the brightness comfortable and the whole painting visible; a television is still a lit screen, so its appearance changes with daylight and reflections.

Confirm that your TV supports USB slideshows and follow its manufacturer’s long-duration display guidance. The slideshow is not an automatic screensaver on every model.

2. Give the television a considered place

Look at the arrangement around it. A console proportionate to the screen, tidier cables and a few objects with varied heights can help the television feel intentional. Nearby framed art can connect the wall to the rest of the room rather than leaving one isolated rectangle.

Keep ventilation openings clear and avoid placing fabric or decorative materials against the television. If changing a wall mount or furniture position, follow the manufacturer’s installation guidance and use appropriate support.

3. Make an intentional off-screen choice

Sometimes the right answer is to leave the TV off and change what surrounds it. A darker wall or backing can reduce its contrast; a cabinet designed for the device can make closing it away part of the room’s routine. Check dimensions, airflow and access before buying furniture for this purpose.

Leaving the TV off also avoids the electricity used by a photo slideshow. Ordinary USB playback is not necessarily an energy-saving Art Mode, and a USB cannot add the hardware features of a dedicated art television.

Start small and judge from the sofa

Try one change before buying new equipment. View the room from the seats you actually use, at different times of day.
