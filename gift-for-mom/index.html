A unique gift for Mom who has everything

Give Mom 1,000+ captioned classic paintings for a compatible TV. $49 once, no subscription. Check compatibility first.

Give Mom a new way to enjoy a familiar room

Some of the most thoughtful gifts fit into a life that is already full. Family Gallery turns a compatible television into a changing display of classic paintings. It adds beauty and a small invitation to learn, using the screen already in the room.

For $49, the prepared USB brings together 1,000+ captioned works. Each painting names its artist and title, so recognizing a favorite or discovering a new artist does not require an art-history background.

Beauty without another monthly bill

The collection is purchased once and stored locally. There is no Family Gallery subscription, app, account or advertising in the artwork. A compatible TV reads the pictures directly from the drive. It is a practical choice for someone who enjoys beautiful surroundings and would appreciate a gift with no recurring Family Gallery charge.

Make the first viewing part of the gift

  1. Check the recipient’s TV model for USB JPEG slideshow support before ordering. A USB port on its own is not enough.
  2. Help insert the drive, find the photo player and start the slideshow.
  3. Choose a comfortable interval and brightness, then leave a short note showing how to start it again.
  4. Spend a little time together picking favorite landscapes, portraits or colors.

For Mother’s Day, a birthday or a holiday, pair the USB with a handwritten note about a painting that reminds you of her.

A gift for curious people of any age

This is an educational art collection for everyday enjoyment. It is welcoming to adults who missed the opportunity to study art history and to families who want to discover artists together. The selection focuses on classic European, Russian and American art created before the 1910s, without nudity or gore.

Before you buy

Check compatibility and preview the collection. A compatible digital photo frame can be another way to enjoy the files if a television lacks a USB photo player. Playback controls depend on the device. Current delivery estimates, shipping charges and any taxes appear on Etsy.
