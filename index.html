Common questions

Before you order.

Is the USB already formatted for my TV?

Yes. Every Family Gallery USB arrives preformatted in FAT32, a widely supported format for TV USB playback, with the artwork already loaded. You do not need to learn how to format a USB for a TV that supports FAT32 USB JPEG slideshows. Check your model’s USB photo playback support before ordering.

What is the exchange policy?

Exchanges are accepted within 7 days of delivery. Buyers are responsible for return shipping costs and any loss in value if the item is not returned in its original condition. See the Etsy listing for the full policy and contact the shop to arrange an exchange.

Do I need a special art television?

No. The USB is intended for televisions that can display JPEG photos from a USB drive. Check your television manual or USB/media menu if you are unsure.

Is there a subscription?

No. It is a one-time physical purchase through Etsy. There is no Family Gallery app or recurring subscription.

Are the paintings labeled?

Yes. Each included image has a caption bar showing the artwork title and artist.
