# The Family Gallery — Etsy shop kit

Paste this into Etsy. Do not upload the painting ZIP to Etsy (20 MB cap per file). Sell the USB as a physical item. Sell the download as a digital listing that delivers a short PDF with a private download link.

## 1. Open the shop (you must do this in a browser)

1. Go to https://www.etsy.com/sell
2. Sign in or create an account.
3. Choose **United States** (or your country), currency USD.
4. Shop name ideas (pick one; Etsy must accept it as unused):
   - TheFamilyGallery
   - FamilyGalleryTV
   - QuietGalleryHome
5. Add a bank account + credit card for fees.
6. Add a production partner only if someone else loads sticks. If you load them, leave that blank.
7. Creativity tags:
   - USB listing: **Made by you** (you assemble and load the stick).
   - Download listing: **Designed by you** (your compilation, captions, crop, and TV format). Be ready for Etsy to question PD-art bundles. Say clearly that buyers get *your curated, captioned, TV-ready files*, not a claim of copyright in Vermeer.
