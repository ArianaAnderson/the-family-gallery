The Family Gallery TV — Make Life Beautiful

Ariana Anderson created The Family Gallery TV to make classic art more accessible using the television already in the room. The idea grew from a practical question: why replace a working screen simply to enjoy paintings? The resulting prepared USB collection pairs classic artwork with artist and title captions, inviting families and adults to discover art at home without a Family Gallery subscription.
