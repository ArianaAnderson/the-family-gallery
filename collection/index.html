Explore 1,000+ captioned classic works from Van Gogh, Monet, Vermeer, Degas, Constable and Bierstadt on one prepared USB.

Recognize favorites. Meet new artists.

Family Gallery brings classic European, Russian and American painting into everyday rooms. Each image includes the title and artist. A landscape can become the start of a conversation; a familiar portrait can lead to a new name or a new period of art.

The complete painting matters

We preserve a work’s original proportions rather than cropping or stretching it to fill a wide screen. Coordinated backgrounds accommodate different canvas shapes. Use your TV’s Fit or Original setting so the caption and edges remain visible.

What guides the selection

The collection focuses on works created before the 1910s, with no nudity or gore. Landscapes, portraits, still lifes and scenes of everyday life make it suitable for shared spaces and family conversations. Captions make the collection educational without requiring a separate guide.

Prepared for you, including the formatting

Preformatted in FAT32, ready for your TV. We format the USB for you in FAT32, a widely supported format for TV USB playback, and preload the artwork. For TVs that support FAT32 USB JPEG slideshows, there is no formatting or file setup to figure out—just plug it in and open your TV’s photo player.

Make it your own

The files are stored locally, so you control the art you display. Your device determines whether you can select folders, change intervals, shuffle or repeat. See the setup guide, and confirm USB JPEG slideshow support before ordering.
